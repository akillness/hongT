# Context: Abyssal Lantern — Cinder Court, per-dungeon gimmick trends

## Workflow Context
Cycle 2 adds new dungeons on top of six existing stages. Each new dungeon needs one signature environmental gimmick that (a) differs mechanically from ember-vent / obsidian-pillar / relic-altar, (b) passes the G8 novelty gate (appears in <=2 of >=5 surveyed titles), and (c) survives the hard runtime constraints: deterministic 60Hz pure-C# sim (NO RNG), data-driven placement, fixed 1536x1024 combat plane with iso distance dy*1.42, WebGL budget (16.67 ms/frame, no compute shaders/threads). Design currently proposes gimmicks from genre memory; this survey replaces that with a per-title mechanical inventory and a frequency table so proposals arrive at G8 pre-scored. (provenance: repo docs `_workspace/current/design/deep-interview-cinder-court-dungeon-revival.md`, direct page retrieval)

How the surveyed genre actually works, per title:

- **Hades** — Biomes differ by a signature spatial rule, not skin: Tartarus arms floor traps the player can lure enemies into; Asphodel replaces floor with lava islands (DoT while standing, dash to cross; explosive barrels, vision-obscuring smoke vents); Elysium adds the Exalted respawn rule (destroy the floating weapon or the enemy revives); Styx switches to cramped tunnel hubs with spinning saws, ceiling cleavers, poison pools that persist until cleansed at a fountain. Traps are largely symmetric — luring foes into Tartarus/Styx traps is core tactics; lava mostly punishes the player since local enemies are immune. Telegraphs: hazard surfaces are permanently visible (lava glow, saw paths); scaling is by density + lethality per biome. (provenance: indexed snippet — fandom.com, techraptor.net, steelseries.com, gamepressure.com, reddit.com; symmetric-trap-luring detail thin evidence)
- **Hades II** — Erebus scatters strikeable dead trees that emit directional shockwaves damaging enemy groups (player-exploitable prop); Oceanus adds water pumps that stun, steam vents, explosive barriers; Fields of Mourning switches structure itself: large open fields with multiple sub-encounters, pits emitting slowing/debuff clouds, thorny damage bushes. Gimmicks range from player-risk (pits, thorns) to player-weapon (shock trees). Scaling is structural: later biome changes the room-flow rule, not just numbers. (provenance: indexed snippet — search-aggregated with citations)
- **Dead Cells** — Biome identity = hazard + economy rule: Toxic Sewers floor poison pools; Ossuary cursed chests (1-HP curse until N kills) and elite density; every transition has timed doors (2-min / 8-min thresholds) that reward speed with loot. Hazards mostly hurt the player (poison-heal amulet inverts it); curse is opt-in risk-reward. Scaling = biome tier + stricter timer. (provenance: indexed snippet)
- **Vampire Survivors** — Stages differ by geometry constraint + scheduled events, almost no floor hazards: Inlaid Library is a horizontal corridor, Gallo Tower a vertical shaft, Dairy Plant a non-linear obstacle field with pressure-plate traps; fixed-time events (Giant Skeleton rush at 26:00, Reaper at 30:00) act as deterministic timed objectives. Player-risk only. Telegraphing is via the clock, not decals. (provenance: indexed snippet — gamerant.com, thegamer.com, namu.wiki)
- **Halls of Torment** — Ember Grounds: lava rivers/patches + on-death fire pools; Forgotten Viaduct: narrow bridge plus indestructible ghost walls that sweep across and shrink the playable area on a timer; Frozen Depths: fast swarms + a map-secret chain (Frost Covered Pages → Hating Heart) that debuffs the stage boss. Hazards player-risk only; scaling via stage shape + timeline pressure. (provenance: indexed snippet)
- **Diablo IV (Nightmare rifts)** — Dungeon variety is injected by sigil afflictions layered onto any tileset: Volcanic (periodic ground eruptions near the player, ground-heat tell), Lightning Storm (strike overhead, forces standing in a spawned safe dome), Drifting Shade (chasing projectile that explodes into a dazing pool). All player-only harassment; scaling = sigil tier raises affliction count/severity. Widely complained about as "anti-chill". (provenance: indexed snippet — blizzard.com, maxroll.gg, mythicdrop.com, reddit.com)
- **Path of Exile (maps/leagues)** — Stage mechanics are data-layered: map mods (burning/chilled/shocked ground, -max res) act as environmental hazards; league encounters are opt-in placed objects — Ritual (fight waves inside a circle for tribute), Blight (tower-defense lanes to a pump), Breach (expanding kill-to-sustain circle), Delirium (fog that recedes behind you, deeper = harder + richer). Layout choice (open vs choke) is the player's difficulty lever. Mostly player-risk; Ritual/Breach circles constrain both sides spatially. (provenance: indexed snippet)
- **Enter the Gungeon** — Room-scale interactive vocabulary shared across chambers: pits (kill enemies too), flippable tables (cover + bullet-clear, enemies flip them as well), fire/poison/acid goop (fire/poison symmetric, acid player-only), water barrels that neutralize goop and electrify puddles, minecart rails with turret carts in Black Powder Mine (chamber-signature mover). Strongly symmetric sandbox; telegraph = persistent visible objects. (provenance: indexed snippet)
- **Binding of Isaac: Repentance** — Chapter-signature gimmicks: Downpour water pools act as player pits but not for aquatic enemies, storm-lit reflections reveal Wraiths; Mines button-repaired minecart track + dark rooms + Mom's-Shadow chase; Mausoleum entry toll of 2 heart containers (cursed door) + in-room teleporters. Mix of player-risk and reveal/traversal tools; scaling by chapter depth + curses. (provenance: indexed snippet)
- **Moonlighter** — Per-dungeon hazard palettes over one structure (3 floors each): Golem = intro traps; Forest = toxic floor patches (DoT); Desert = similar DoT patches, erratic bosses; Tech = taser/stasis traps. Overstaying spawns the invincible Wanderer chaser. Player-risk only; scaling = later dungeon + deeper floor. (provenance: indexed snippet)
- **Death Must Die** — Act variety is mostly enemy-composition (Act 1 Graveyard horde vs Act 2 Endless Desert elite/ranged pressure) rather than placed environmental gimmicks; difficulty dial is the Darkness system. Weakest environmental-gimmick vocabulary in the pool — useful as a negative control. (provenance: indexed snippet; thin evidence for absence claims)

## Current Workarounds
1. Designers propose gimmicks from genre memory ("Hades does lava, so lava") — biases toward the MOST saturated archetypes, exactly the ones G8 rejects.
2. The existing trio was chosen ad hoc: ember-vent (= genre-standard periodic AoE), obsidian-pillar (= genre-standard blocker), relic-altar (= genre-standard stand-in buff zone) — all three land in the top-frequency band of this survey, confirming the habit.
3. RNG-flavored references (Isaac room rolls, PoE map mods) get manually "determinized" per proposal with no shared recipe; each proposal re-argues how to strip RNG instead of reusing a fixed-schedule/fixed-placement pattern.
4. Novelty is currently argued qualitatively in review ("feels fresh") rather than counted against a title matrix; G8 decisions were not reproducible before this artifact.

## Adjacent Problems
- Calibration (telegraph duration, damage as % of player HP, ramp curves) — owned by the parallel QA benchmark survey; the two tables must stay joinable on the shared 6-title pool (Hades, Dead Cells, VS, Halls of Torment, Gungeon, Diablo).
- Enemy AI interaction: symmetric gimmicks (both sides take damage/push) require enemy pathfinding and steering to respect hazard zones — a sim cost this survey flags per candidate but does not solve.
- Data-driven placement schema: several candidates need new placement fields (phase offset, lane path, pair links); schema ownership sits with the sim cartographer lane (`MapStageSystems`).
- Reduced-motion / accessibility fallbacks for moving hazards and vision-limit gimmicks (repo presentation contract already mandates reduced-motion-safe beats).
